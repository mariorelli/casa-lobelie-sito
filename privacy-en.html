Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data we collect through the Casa Lobelie website, how it is used, and what rights you have regarding it.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data controller

The controller of the data collected through this website is Mario, host of Casa Lobelie, Via delle Lobelie 20, Rome (CIN IT058091C2ODNCG79B). For any request regarding your data, you can contact us directly on WhatsApp, at the number published on the site.

2. What data we collect

When you use the “Request Availability” form, we collect:

  • full name
  • email address
  • phone number / WhatsApp (optional)
  • check-in and check-out dates
  • number of guests

This data is provided voluntarily by you when submitting the request. When a booking is made through the Smoobu booking engine, similar data (and any payment information) is collected and processed as described in the dedicated section below.

3. How the data is used

The data collected is used exclusively to respond to your availability request, communicate the rate and stay conditions, and manage any resulting direct booking. We do not use your data for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

4. How the data is transmitted

The request sent through the website's form is delivered to our email address via Web3Forms, a third-party provider that only handles the delivery of the message and does not retain or use your data for its own purposes. Alternatively, you can choose to send your request directly via WhatsApp.

5. Third-party services on this site

The site embeds a Google Maps map in the “The Area” section and uses Google Fonts typefaces. Using these services may involve transmitting some technical data, such as your IP address, to Google's servers, in accordance with Google's own privacy policies.

6. Smoobu and payments via Stripe

Direct bookings made through the Smoobu booking engine are managed through that platform, which processes the availability calendar and the data required for the booking (name, email, phone, stay dates, number of guests). Payments for these bookings are securely processed by Stripe, a specialised online payment provider. Casa Lobelie does not directly store your full payment card data, which remains handled exclusively by Stripe in accordance with its own security and privacy policies.

7. Cookies

This site does not use its own profiling or marketing cookies. Embedded third-party services, such as Google Maps, may set their own technical cookies when you interact with them, in accordance with Google's policies.

8. Data retention

Data collected through the request form is kept only for as long as necessary to handle your request and any resulting stay, except where retention is required by law — for example, reporting guest data to the relevant authorities, as required by Italian short-term rental regulations.

9. Your rights

As a data subject, you have the right to access your data, request its correction or deletion, object to its processing, and request its portability, within the limits set out in Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR). To exercise these rights, you can contact us on WhatsApp, at the number published on the site.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. We encourage you to check this page periodically.

This document is provided for general information purposes and does not replace personalised legal or privacy advice. We recommend having it reviewed by a professional to ensure full regulatory compliance.
