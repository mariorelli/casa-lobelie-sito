Welcome

A quiet retreat in the heart of East Rome

Casa Lobelie is a peaceful oasis in the Alessandrino district, a genuine residential corner of East Rome, away from the noise and traffic of the historic centre. Quiet comes standard here: in the evening you can unwind on your own private veranda, a retreat where time slows down and the day's sightseeing becomes a memory to savour at leisure — far from the tourist bustle, yet never far from Rome.

The interiors are carefully finished down to the last detail: a comfortable bedroom — set up with either a double bed or two twin beds — with air conditioning and independent heating for every season, a full bathroom, a well-equipped kitchen, and the private veranda, the true heart of the home: quiet and furnished, perfect for breakfast or an evening drink when all you want is to switch off from the city's chaos.

Where We Are

Alessandrino District, East Rome

A quiet, genuine neighbourhood, away from the noise and traffic of the historic centre — ideal for real relaxation after a day among the monuments. The historic centre is still just a step away, easily reachable by public transport.

  • Bus line 213 — stop about 100 metres from the apartment
  • Metro C nearby — straight to the Colosseum in about 30 minutes
  • Central Rome easily reachable by public transport
  • Shops, cafés and neighbourhood services close at hand
  • Restaurants and pizzerias just a short walk away, no car needed for dinner
  • A large, well-stocked, budget-friendly supermarket just steps from the door
