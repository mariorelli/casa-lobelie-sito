Relaxation and privacy for two

Casa Lobelie is a private apartment designed for a maximum of 2 guests, ideal for those looking for a quiet, secluded stay in Rome. The private veranda and wellness area let you carve out a moment of relaxation away from the pace of the city, while keeping Rome within easy reach.

Private veranda and wellness area
